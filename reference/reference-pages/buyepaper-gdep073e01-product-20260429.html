7.3" Color E Ink Spectra™ 6 Display

Transform your displays with the vibrant and versatile 7.3-inch E Ink Spectra 6 screen. Featuring six stunning colors—this display brings your ideas to life with unparalleled clarity and precision.  ​​​​​​

Spectra™ 6 Color Revolution

64% wider color gamut than previous generation technology
 

 

Spectra™ 6

  • 4096 vibrant colors
  • 85% NTSC coverage
  • 300% higher saturation
VS
 

Previous Generation

  • Limited color range
  • 50% NTSC coverage
  • Standard saturation

Key Features

6-Color Imaging System

Advanced color rendering with black, white, red, green, blue, and yellow pigments. 127.8 PPI with 800×480 pixel density for crystal-clear content.

Enhanced Dithering Algorithms

The ESP32E6-E01 driver board provides a complete Wi-Fi/Bluetooth communication solution. The accompanying software includes multiple dithering algorithms, making display optimization straightforward for users.

Advanced Partial Refresh

The display refreshes only modified regions instead of the entire screen, reducing unnecessary updates, lowering energy use, and minimizing visible flicker.
