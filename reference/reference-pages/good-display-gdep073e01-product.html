E Ink Display's Basic Parameters 

 

  • Product size: 7.3 inch

  • Resolution: 800x480

  • PPI: 127.8

  • Interface: SPI (50PIN)

  • Operating voltage: 3.3V

  • Operating temperature: 0-50 ℃

  • Color: 6 color(E Ink Spectra 6)

 

 

Spectra™ 6 E Ink Display

Revolutionary six-color e-ink technology redefining digital display standards - clearer, greener, more economical   

Revolutionary Cost-Saving Solution

Spectra™ 6 consumes zero power in static display mode with extended lifespan, significantly reducing total cost of ownership (TCO)

Six-Color HD Display Technology

Spectra™ 6 breaks traditional e-ink limitations with six-color high-resolution display, 300% sharper images and text comparable to print quality.

Eye-Friendly & Environmentally Safe

7.3-inch large screen display with paper-like reading experience, completely eliminating blue light hazards and screen flickering.
